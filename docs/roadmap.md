# Roadmap

This roadmap describes the intended delivery order. Each phase follows the Documentation First lifecycle described in the README.

1. **Phase 0 — Repository & Documentation:** establish public-safe repository conventions, architecture, security guidance, and development documentation.
2. **Phase 1 — Platform Core:** define shared data model, environment configuration, operational foundations, and workflow conventions.
3. **Phase 2 — Cybersecurity Sources:** ingest selected cybersecurity intelligence sources.
4. **Phase 3 — Deduplication, AI Scoring & Research:** identify duplicate items, score relevance, and verify research sources.
5. **Phase 4 — LinkedIn Content Agent:** produce evidence-based LinkedIn content drafts from approved intelligence.
6. **Phase 5 — Human Approval & Publishing:** add approval records and approved publishing integration.
7. **Phase 6 — Comment Agent:** monitor comments and generate human-approved reply suggestions.
8. **Phase 7 — Analytics:** measure pipeline, content, and engagement outcomes.
9. **Phase 8 — Security Hardening & CI/CD:** strengthen security controls and delivery automation.
10. **Phase 9 — VPS Migration:** document and execute deployment to a managed VPS environment.
11. **Phase 10 — Future Platform Extensions:** evaluate lead generation, CRM, email, and WhatsApp modules.

Future phases may be reprioritized, but they must not weaken human approval or public-repository safety.
